Under development-Fight to unify Terra and then the Sol system as the Emperor of Mankind, or search for humanity's lost technologic heritage as the Mechanicum of Mars in this simulation of the Unification Wars of the 30th millennium. 

So I decided to get the ball rolling on this mod concept, as myself and many others feel that TI has a great deal of potential for this kind of setting. It has intrigue, nation building and conquest, void combat, exploration of the unknown, and an alien menace looming in the dark. The current build of the mod is more of a proof of concept for several mechanics, such as renaming Earth to Terra, habs located on Terra, renaming and reshaping national polities, new factions and ideologies, and new hab modules. There are still many blockers for mechanics I wish to pursue, such as transferring between a region and habs on the same planet, and the general mechanic of setting up and triggering narrative events. I could use help in several areas:

[b][u]Compatibility[/u][/b]
This is an overhaul mod that modifies most everything about Terra Invicta. Due to the current way that merging .json files work, most files will merge and overwrite, but not remove, vanilla files and entries. So if you are using any other mods that rely on vanilla entries, those will most likely not work. 
The mod requires Unity Mod Manager to function. It now (as of 8 October 2022) has support for Terra Invicta!

To install Unity Mod Manager, download version 0.25.0 or later version from Nexus Mods [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/site/mods/21]https://www.nexusmods.com/site/mods/21[/url].
Version Information:
I am running the standard stable Steam release, (last updated TI build 0.3.35), and UMM 0.25.0.0. I have all my mod data under \Terra Invicta\Mods\Enabled\UnificationWars, including UnificationWars.dll.
